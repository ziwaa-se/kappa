## 2. Faithfulness

Definitions first. The paper defines $V_n(p)=p(1-p)/n$ for real $p$ with $n\ge1$ an integer, and
$s^2(u)=\frac1{m-1}\sum(u_i-\bar u)^2$ with $\bar u=\frac1m\sum u_i$ for $m\ge2$.

- **Kit** (`Defs.lean`): `V n p := p * (1 - p) / (n : ℝ)`; `mean u := (1 / (m : ℝ)) * ∑ i, u i`;
  `s2 u := (1 / ((m : ℝ) - 1)) * ∑ i, (u i - mean u) ^ 2`; `IOpen := Set.Ioo 0 1`; `IClosed := Set.Icc 0 1`. All match.
  The conventions $n\ge1$ and $m\ge2$ appear as hypotheses in the theorems.
- **Agent:** `V n p := p * (1 - p) / n`; `mean u := (∑ i, u i) / m`; `svar u := (∑ i, (u i - mean u) ^ 2) / (m - 1)`.
  `#print svar` shows `/ (↑m - 1)` in ℝ, so there is no natural-number truncation. All match.
- **Chat:** `V`, `mean` and `s2` as in the Agent, with an explicit `((m : ℝ) - 1)`. All match.

`x + y` on `Fin m → ℝ` is pointwise addition, as in the paper. The paper leaves the reading of $I$ open, so a
statement under either reading counts as faithful when the arm discloses the reading.

## 3. Claims outside the reference

Each claim was checked by hand, and in Lean where the arm or the grader had a file.

### Kit

1. **"On (0,1) it holds exactly for p ≥ 3/4" and "it fails for every n ≥ 1 at p = 1/10"** (B3, report §4.2).
   *Correct.* For $p>0$, $p(1-p)\le p/4 \iff 1-p\le\frac14$. In Lean these are `open_iff` and `fails_every_n` in
   `L03_Counterexample1.lean`, which compile with standard axioms.
2. **"The identity is false for every m ≥ 2", and "reading '=' as '≤' fails at the same instance"** (B2).
   *Correct.* For $x=y$, $s^2(2x)=4s^2(x)$, which differs from $2s^2(x)$ unless $s^2(x)=0$. At $(0,1)$ this gives
   $2>1$. In Lean: `fails_every_m` and `counterexample_le`.
3. **"Fixing I = [0,1] at main.tex:24 would not suffice; restrict Lemma 2.2 to (0,1)", and "a half-open reading
   fails at the included endpoint"** (B1, report §6). *Correct*, since $V_n(0)=V_n(1)=0$.
4. **"[Proposition 2.5 is] sharp (every working C is ≥ 1/4)"** (report §2; a probe in `P02_final.md`). *Correct.*
   At $p=\frac12$, $\frac1{4n}\le C/n$ forces $C\ge\frac14$. This is a positive claim beyond the paper, and it is true.
5. **Suggested fixes.** For 2.4: (a) the bound $p/n$, (b) restricting to $[3/4,1]$, (c) deleting the lemma. For 2.6: the
   bound $s^2(x+y)=s^2(x)+s^2(y)+2c$, with $2c\le s^2(x)+s^2(y)$. *All correct.* (a) holds because
   $p(1-p)\le p \iff p^2\ge0$; (b) follows from item 1; the 2.6 fix follows from $2\sum a_ib_i\le\sum a_i^2+\sum b_i^2$.
   These are labelled as reasoning only.
6. **"The 'well known' concavity is a FAIR_IMPLICIT_STEP"** (P01 gate and final audit). This is a classification
   judgement that differs from the key's (ii), not a problem claim. HAND_CHECKS records that the kit's rules allow it.
   Not a false alarm.
7. **Coordinator observation: "Proposition 2.5 repeats 'for every n ≥ 1'"**. *Correct* as an observation, and it is
   explicitly not presented as a defect.

There are no false alarms.
